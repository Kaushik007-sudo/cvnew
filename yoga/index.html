About me

A life shaped by devotion and service

Yoga Instructor Wellness Coach Spiritual Healer

Born in Kolkata — the very city where Śrīla A. C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda began his own journey — Amita Balaram Das grew up close to the roots of the Vedic tradition he now shares with students across the world.

He serves as an active leader, service devotee and community organiser with ISKCON Atlanta, and is the Director at Food4Lives, where care for the body and care for the soul meet through community seva.

His teaching draws on formal study in the United States and years of dedicated training in India, bringing together modern wellness thinking and the classical science of yoga.

  • S-VYASA Yoga University, Bangalore, IndiaAdvanced training in yoga science and practice
  • Kidee SchoolBachelor of Science in Yoga
  • Gwinnett College, Georgia, USAUndergraduate studies
