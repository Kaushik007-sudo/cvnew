02 • PM BASICS

Understand the four delivery approaches

Use these simple definitions in an interview.

AGILE

Flexible delivery

Deliver small pieces, get feedback, and adapt as requirements change.

Best when: requirements may change
SCRUM

Agile framework

Organize work into short sprints with a backlog, goals, reviews and regular team discussions.

Think: Sprint + Backlog + Review
WATERFALL

Step-by-step

Complete one phase before moving to the next, with a more fixed plan.

Best when: requirements are stable
KANBAN

Continuous flow

Visualize work such as To Do → In Progress → Done and limit work in progress.

Think: Flow + WIP limits
🧠
REMEMBER THIS

Agile is the approach. Scrum is a framework.

Waterfall is sequential. Kanban focuses on continuous flow.

Does everyone know progress, blockers, decisions and next steps?
